The full journey — not just the form

The payment logic is only one piece. Here's every stage the parent and player moves through, what they see, and which platform handles it.

🏠

1. Landing page — before the form

Most parents won't fill in a form cold. The landing page does the selling first: what the program is, what it costs per quarter, what's included, and how billing works. Each program card is clickable and pre-selects that group in the form, so the parent never has to re-choose what they already clicked.

Your site / Jotform page Pre-selects group in form
📝

2. Registration form — fast and visual

Three short steps instead of a long page. Big tappable cards instead of dropdowns for the two decisions that matter (group and player type). Medical and emergency details sit in a collapsed optional section so they don't slow anyone down, but are still there for the families who need them.

Jotform Conditional logic
💳

3. Payment — first charge differs from the rest

Existing players: one quarterly amount. New players: the same quarterly amount plus a one-off $300 kit fee on this first payment only. Both go through a single Stripe checkout session — one card entry, one invoice, no double charging. The card is saved to the customer for every future payment.

Stripe Checkout One-time + recurring in one session
✅

4. Immediately after payment — confirmation screen

The parent lands on a success page with their receipt, their payment schedule, and — crucially — what happens next and when. A welcome screen that just says "payment received" leaves parents anxious. This one tells them: welcome email now, coach call within 48 hours, kit within 5 days, first session on X date.

Jotform thank-you page Stripe receipt
📧

5. Automated emails — the invisible follow-up

Stripe sends the payment receipt automatically. Jotform sends the registration confirmation to the parent and a notification to the academy. A separate welcome email covers kit collection, first session details and who to contact. This is what stops the academy office getting the same phone call twenty times.

Jotform autoresponder Stripe receipts
🔁

6. Months 3, 6 and 9 — automatic renewals

No action from the parent. Stripe charges the saved card every 3 months. These invoices contain only the quarterly subscription amount — the kit fee never appears again because it was created as a one-time price. Smart Retries handle a failed card and prompt the parent to update it.

Stripe Billing Kit fee absent
🏁

7. Month 12 — the subscription ends itself

After the 4th payment the subscription cancels automatically. No 5th charge, no manual admin, nothing for the academy to remember. If they want to re-enrol for the next season, that's a fresh registration — which is cleaner for reporting anyway.

Stripe cancel_at No admin needed

Requirement → implementation

#RequirementHow it's built
1 Quarterly recurring subscription Stripe recurring Price with interval = month, interval_count = 3. The cadence is owned by Stripe, not the form.
2 Different amount per group One Product + recurring Price per group. The group cards in step 2 map to those prices — only one can be selected.
3 Existing player — normal first payment Selecting "Existing player" hides the kit fee product and resets its value to zero, so the first invoice is subscription only.
4 New player — first payment + $300 Selecting "New player" reveals the one-time $300 product. Both line items submit in the same Stripe checkout session.
5 Kit fee must not recur The kit fee is a one-time Price with no recurring attribute — structurally unable to recur. It lands on invoice #1 only.
6 12-month / 4-payment term cancel_at set to start + 12 months, or a 4-phase Subscription Schedule with end_behavior: cancel for a strict 4-payment cap.
7 Card saved for future charges Subscription-mode Checkout creates the Customer and attaches the card as default payment method. Smart Retries enabled.
8 Collect registration, medical, consents, payment Single Jotform with three steps. Medical is optional-but-available; consents are bundled into one required agreement plus an optional media toggle.
9 Clean logic, no duplicate charges One payment field only. Conditions toggle visibility and reset values — they never create a second charge path. Submit button guarded against double-clicks.
10 Full testing before go-live Stripe test mode for every group/player-type combination, plus Test Clocks to fast-forward 12 months and prove the 4-payment cap and end date.
11 Confirm first payment, future payments, end date Handover evidence: invoice #1 showing both lines, invoices #2–4 showing subscription only, and the subscription's "Cancels on" date.
12 Simplify + handover Audit existing fields, merge duplicates, collapse optional sections. Then a written guide plus a short walkthrough recording of Stripe and Jotform.

What makes this safe

  • One payment field. Conditions change visibility, never charge count.
  • Kit fee is a one-time price. It cannot recur even if form logic breaks later.
  • The term is enforced by Stripe. No one has to remember to cancel anything.
  • Double-submit protection. One registration = one subscription, always.

Decisions needed before the build

  • After 12 months: hard stop, or auto-renew for next season? (Currently built as hard stop.)
  • Failed payments: how many retries before the academy is notified?
  • Kit collection: posted, collected in person, or at first session?
  • Refunds: what's the policy if a player leaves mid-term?
